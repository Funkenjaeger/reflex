Sound Volume
============

This setting controls the volume of the audible feedback (beeps). The
DRO beeps when it reaches a target position or confirms an action.

## Usage

- Drag the slider to set the volume (0–100%)
- Press "Test" to hear a sample beep at the current volume
- To mute all sounds, set the volume to 0

## Notes

- The sound plays through the system's default audio output
- In a noisy workshop, a higher volume lets you confirm operations
  without looking at the screen
