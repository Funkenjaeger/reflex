## reflex v1.2.0-rc.6

The firmware-update fix: in-app updates stalled part-way through because the
bootloader ran on the chip's internal oscillator, which on the lathe measured
3.6% slow. The bootloader now runs on the board's crystal. Also a more patient
updater, a status box that scrolls, and a verified recovery step for the
operator. Same register protocol as rc.5 (11).

### ⚠ Upgrade notes
- **The bootloader fix does not install from the touchscreen.** The bootloader
  lives in a protected flash sector that only a programmer (SWD) can write;
  the `reflex-bl` asset here is the fixed one. See `fw/bootloader/README.md`,
  steps 4 and 9b. The development lathe already has it.

### Fixes
- **Bootloader** runs on the 8 MHz crystal instead of the internal RC
  oscillator, and reports which clock it is on. Measured on the lathe: a
  transfer that failed after 265 s now takes 12 s with no retries.
- **In-app update:** if a transfer fails, the updater keeps trying for up to
  150 s to put the previous firmware back (it gave up after 30 s); progress
  reaches the status box as it happens; if the controller still cannot be
  recovered, the first instruction is to turn the machine off, wait 10
  seconds and turn it on (tested on the lathe).
- **Update screen:** the status box scrolls on touch instead of opening the
  keyboard.

### Verified
Firmware, UI and system CI green on the promoted commit. The bootloader fix,
the recovery step and the updater's recovery were exercised on the
development lathe on 2026-09-24; installing this release from the
touchscreen is its final check.
